Section 01

Introduction

GoGlobalMart Mini Store ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website and mini-store platform accessible at goglobalmart.com. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, process, and disclose your information, including personal information, in conjunction with your access to and use of our platform.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about our data practices. This policy applies to all users of our services, including visitors, registered customers, and account holders.

By accessing or using our platform, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to be bound by the terms of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this policy, please do not access our platform.

Important Notice

This policy is effective as of July 12, 2025. We reserve the right to update this policy at any time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Section 02

Data Collection

We collect information that you provide directly to us, information collected automatically when you use our platform, and information from third-party sources. The types of data we collect include:

Personal Information

Full name, email address, phone number, shipping and billing addresses, date of birth (when required for age verification).

Payment Information

Credit/debit card details (processed securely by payment providers), billing address, transaction history, and order details.

Device & Technical Data

IP address, browser type and version, operating system, device identifiers, screen resolution, and language preferences.

Usage & Behavioral Data

Pages viewed, click patterns, time spent on pages, search queries, products browsed, and shopping cart activity.

We collect personal information when you create an account, place an order, subscribe to our newsletter, contact customer support, participate in promotions, or leave product reviews.

We do not collect special categories of personal data (such as health data, biometric data, or political opinions) unless explicitly required for a specific service and with your explicit consent.

Section 03

Cookies Usage

Our platform uses cookies and similar tracking technologies (including pixel tags, web beacons, and local storage) to enhance your browsing experience, analyze traffic, and personalize content. Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit our website.

Cookie Type Purpose Duration
Strictly Necessary Enable core functionality like shopping cart, checkout, and secure login Session / 30 days
Performance Collect anonymous data about how visitors use the site (e.g., Google Analytics) Up to 2 years
Functionality Remember your preferences, language, and region settings Up to 1 year
Targeting / Marketing Deliver relevant advertisements and track campaign effectiveness Up to 2 years

You can manage your cookie preferences at any time through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to refuse or delete cookies. However, disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of our platform, and some features may not work as intended.

We also use pixel tags and web beacons in our email communications to track open rates and click-through rates, which helps us improve our communications with you.

Section 04

Payment Security

Protecting your financial information is a top priority. GoGlobalMart Mini Store implements industry-leading security measures to ensure that your payment data is processed safely and securely.

Our Security Measures Include:

  • PCI DSS Compliance

    We comply with the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI DSS) Level 1 requirements, the highest level of certification available.

  • TLS/SSL Encryption

    All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS) 1.3 protocol with 256-bit AES encryption.

  • Tokenization

    Your credit card number is replaced with a unique token. We never store your full card details on our servers — payment processing is handled entirely by our certified payment partners (Stripe, PayPal).

  • 3D Secure Authentication

    We support 3D Secure 2.0 (3DS2) for an additional layer of verification during online transactions, reducing the risk of unauthorized payments.

  • Fraud Detection

    Automated systems monitor transactions in real-time for suspicious activity, including unusual purchase patterns, IP geolocation mismatches, and velocity checks.

In the event of a data breach that affects your payment information, we will notify you within 72 hours in compliance with applicable data breach notification laws.

Section 05

User Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including the EU under GDPR, California under CCPA/CPRA, and other applicable privacy laws), you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

Right to Access

You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you, including details of how it has been processed and who it has been shared with.

Right to Rectification

You can request that we correct any inaccurate or incomplete personal data we hold about you.

Right to Erasure ("Right to Be Forgotten")

You can request the deletion of your personal data, subject to certain legal exceptions (e.g., ongoing legal obligations, dispute resolution).

Right to Restrict Processing

You can request that we limit the processing of your personal data in certain circumstances, such as when you contest the accuracy of the data.

Right to Data Portability

You can request to receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format (e.g., JSON or CSV) and transmit it to another service provider.

Right to Object

You can object to the processing of your personal data for direct marketing purposes, profiling, or processing based on our legitimate interests.

Right to Withdraw Consent

Where processing is based on your consent, you can withdraw that consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out prior to withdrawal.

Right to Non-Discrimination (CCPA)

We will not discriminate against you for exercising any of your privacy rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act.

To exercise any of these rights, please submit a request to privacy@goglobalmart.com. We will respond to your request within 30 days (or as required by applicable law). We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling your request.

Section 07

Data Protection

We implement robust technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Our data protection framework includes:

Secure Infrastructure

Data hosted on SOC 2 Type II certified cloud infrastructure with automated backups and disaster recovery protocols.

Access Controls

Role-based access control (RBAC), multi-factor authentication for internal systems, and principle of least privilege enforcement.

Regular Audits

Annual security audits, vulnerability scanning, penetration testing, and continuous monitoring for potential threats.

Data Retention: We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law (e.g., tax and accounting records are retained for a minimum of 7 years). Anonymous and aggregated data may be retained indefinitely for analytical purposes.

International Transfers: If your data is transferred outside of your country of residence, we ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place, such as Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission, or transfers to countries deemed to provide an adequate level of data protection.

Section 08

Account Deletion

You have the right to delete your GoGlobalMart Mini Store account at any time. We respect your decision and make the process straightforward.

How to Delete Your Account:

1

Log in to your account

Sign in at goglobalmart.com with your credentials.

2

Navigate to Account Settings

Go to Settings → Privacy → Delete Account.

3

Confirm your decision

Review the impact of deletion and confirm by entering your password.

4

Deletion initiated

You'll receive a confirmation email. Your account enters a 30-day grace period.

Important: What Happens When You Delete Your Account

  • • Your profile, order history, and saved addresses will be permanently removed
  • • Active orders will still be fulfilled, but you won't be able to track them in your account
  • • Wishlists, reviews, and saved payment methods will be deleted
  • • Loyalty points and store credit will be forfeited and cannot be recovered
  • • You will be unsubscribed from all marketing communications
  • • Anonymized purchase data may be retained for tax and legal compliance

Alternatively, you can request account deletion by emailing privacy@goglobalmart.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" from the email address associated with your account.

During the 30-day grace period, you can log back in to cancel the deletion. After 30 days, the deletion is irreversible.
