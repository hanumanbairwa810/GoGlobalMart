Overview

GoGlobal Mart runs three different services, and each one handles delivery differently. Please check which one your order belongs to before you look for help.

Affiliate ShoppingYou buy on an external seller's website. The seller delivers.
Mini StoreYou buy directly from GoGlobal Mart. Fulfilment follows the Mini Store process below.
Food RestroFood orders follow a separate process from product shipping.

This policy should be read together with our Terms of Service, Privacy Policy and Affiliate Disclosure. Nothing here limits any consumer rights you have under applicable law.

Affiliate Shopping: external seller deliveries

GoGlobal Mart shows product information and links that take you to external sellers and marketplaces. If you complete a purchase on a seller's website, that seller or its fulfilment partner generally handles shipping and delivery. GoGlobal Mart does not fulfil these orders.

  • Shipping charges, delivery areas, estimated arrival dates, tracking, customs charges and cancellations are set by the seller's own terms.
  • Prices, availability, shipping charges and delivery estimates can change on the seller's platform without notice to us.
  • For delays, missing parcels, damaged deliveries or tracking problems, contact the seller or marketplace where you placed the order first. Use the order details in your confirmation email or account on that platform.
  • GoGlobal Mart can help with website-related issues, such as a broken link, but we do not control the seller's logistics.

We do not promise free shipping, delivery dates, coverage areas or specific couriers for affiliate purchases.

Contact and support

For shipping help with a GoGlobal Mart order, use the Contact Us page and include:

  • Your order reference.
  • The service: Affiliate Shopping, Mini Store or Food Restro.
  • A short description of the problem.
  • A tracking reference, if you have one.

Never share your password, OTP, full card number or CVV with anyone, including in a support message.
