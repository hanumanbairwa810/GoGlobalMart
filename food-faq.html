Customer FAQs

7 questions

Browse restaurants on our platform, select your favorite items, add them to your cart, proceed to checkout, choose your payment method, and confirm your order. You'll receive real-time updates on your order status.

Steps:

  • Enter your delivery address
  • Browse or search for restaurants
  • Select items and add to cart
  • Review your order and apply coupons
  • Choose payment method and confirm

Once your order is confirmed, you'll receive a tracking link via SMS and email. You can also track your order in real-time through the GoGlobalMart Food app or website under "My Orders".

The tracking shows your order status: Confirmed → Preparing → Picked Up → On the Way → Delivered.

You can cancel your order within 2 minutes of placing it for a full refund. After the restaurant starts preparing your food, cancellation may not be possible. Contact support for assistance with late cancellations.

To cancel: Go to My Orders → Select Order → Cancel Order.

We accept multiple payment methods for your convenience:

  • Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex)
  • Digital Wallets (Apple Pay, Google Pay)
  • Net Banking
  • UPI Payments
  • GoGlobalMart Wallet
  • Cash on Delivery (select locations)

Refunds are initiated within 24 hours of a valid request. The amount is credited back to your original payment method within 5–7 business days. Wallet refunds are processed instantly.

You can track refund status under My Orders → Order Details → Refund Status.

If your order exceeds the estimated delivery time by more than 15 minutes, you can contact support for a resolution which may include a discount coupon or partial refund. We take delivery timeliness seriously.

You can check real-time estimated delivery time in the tracking section of your order.

You can reach us through multiple channels:

  • Live Chat: Available 24/7 on the app and website
  • Email: support@goglobalmart01@gmail.com
  • Phone: 1-800-GOGLOBAL (toll-free)
  • In-App Help: Help section in your profile

Delivery FAQs

Delivery charges vary by location and restaurant, typically ranging from $0.99 to $4.99. Orders above $25 usually qualify for free delivery. The exact delivery fee is displayed at checkout before you confirm your order.

Look for the "Free Delivery" tag on eligible restaurants.

Average delivery time is 25–45 minutes depending on the restaurant, distance, and order volume. You'll see the estimated delivery time before placing your order. During peak hours, delivery may take slightly longer.

We deliver to most urban and suburban areas across 500+ cities. Enter your address on the homepage to check if your location is serviceable. We're constantly expanding our coverage areas.

If your area isn't covered yet, you can request service in your location through the app.

Yes, you can select "Contactless Delivery" at checkout. Your delivery partner will leave the order at your doorstep and send you a photo confirmation, ensuring a safe and hygienic experience.

You can also add delivery instructions like "Leave at the gate" or "Ring the bell and step back."

Our real-time tracking system shows your delivery partner's live location on a map. You'll see updates at every stage — from order confirmation to food preparation, pickup, and final delivery. You can also call or message your delivery partner directly.

Payment & Refund FAQs

Absolutely. We use industry-standard 256-bit SSL encryption and are PCI-DSS compliant. Your payment data is never stored on our servers — it's processed through certified payment gateways. We also support 3D Secure authentication for added safety.

If a transaction fails, the amount is automatically reversed by your bank within 3–5 business days. You can try an alternative payment method. If money was debited but the order wasn't placed, contact support with your transaction ID for quick resolution.

Refund processing times vary by payment method:

  • GoGlobalMart Wallet: Instant
  • UPI: 1–2 business days

If you're facing wallet balance issues, failed top-ups, or payment errors, try these steps:

  • Check your internet connection
  • Ensure sufficient balance in your payment method
  • Try a different payment method
  • Clear app cache and restart

If the issue persists, contact support with a screenshot of the error for fast resolution.

Restaurant Partner FAQs

5 questions

Visit our "Partner With Us" page and fill out the registration form with your restaurant details. Upload required documents (FSSAI license, GST registration, bank details, menu), and our team will review and onboard you within 3–5 business days.

Our approval process includes:

  • Document verification: FSSAI, GST, PAN, bank details
  • Quality check: Kitchen hygiene and food quality audit
  • Menu setup: Upload items with photos, descriptions, pricing
  • Training: Partner app orientation and order management

The entire process typically takes 3–5 business days.

Use the GoGlobalMart Partner App or Partner Dashboard to manage your menu. You can add new items, edit descriptions and prices, upload photos, set availability status, create combo deals, and manage dietary tags (vegan, gluten-free, etc.). Changes go live within minutes.

You'll receive order notifications on the Partner App with details including items, special instructions, and customer info. You can accept or reject orders within a set time. Once accepted, you update the preparation status, and the system notifies the delivery partner for pickup.

Our commission structure is transparent and competitive. Commission rates vary between 15%–25% depending on the plan you choose. We offer tiered pricing based on order volume. Payments are settled weekly directly to your registered bank account with detailed settlement reports.

Account & Security FAQs

4 questions

You can sign up using your email address, phone number, or social accounts (Google, Facebook, Apple). Download the app or visit the website, tap "Sign Up," fill in your details, verify your number/email, and you're ready to order. Existing users can simply log in with their credentials.

Click "Forgot Password" on the login page, enter your registered email or phone number, and follow the verification link sent to you. Create a new password (minimum 8 characters with a number and special character) and you're all set. If you don't receive the link, check your spam folder.

We take your privacy seriously. Your personal data is encrypted and stored securely. We never sell your data to third parties. You can control what data is shared through your account settings. Our practices comply with GDPR, CCPA, and applicable data protection regulations.

Review our full Privacy Policy for detailed information.

We employ multiple layers of security:

  • Two-Factor Authentication (2FA): Optional but recommended
  • Session management: Auto-logout after inactivity
  • Device tracking: View and remove logged-in devices
  • Fraud detection: AI-powered suspicious activity monitoring
  • Secure passwords: Encrypted password storage
