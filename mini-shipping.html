SECTION 01

Delivery Timelines

From the moment you place your order to when it arrives at your doorstep — here's exactly what to expect at every stage.

Instant

Order Confirmed

Your order is placed and confirmed. You'll receive an email with your order number and summary details.

1-2 Business Days

Processing & Packed

Our team carefully picks, quality-checks, and packs your items. Stock verification and secure packaging are completed.

Day 2-3

Shipped & In Transit

Your package is handed to our carrier partner. A tracking number is generated and shared via email and SMS.

Day 4-7

Out for Delivery

Your package arrives at the local delivery hub and is assigned to a delivery agent. You'll get a real-time notification.

Day 5-7

Delivered! 🎉

Your order is delivered to your specified address. A delivery confirmation with photo proof is sent to you.

Please Note

Delivery timelines are estimates based on business days (Mon–Sat, excluding public holidays). Remote areas, high-demand periods (sales, festive seasons), and weather conditions may add 1-3 additional days.

SECTION 05

Delayed Shipment Policy

We understand delays are frustrating. Here's our commitment when things don't go as planned.

1

Delay of 1-3 Days Beyond ETA

No action needed from you. Our system automatically flags the shipment and the carrier is notified. We'll send you a status update within 24 hours.

2

Delay of 4-7 Days Beyond ETA

Our support team proactively reaches out with an investigation update. You may request a 10% shipping fee refund as a goodwill gesture.

3

Delay Exceeding 7 Days Beyond ETA

Full shipping fee refund is issued automatically. You'll also have the option to cancel for a full order refund or continue waiting with an additional 15% store credit.

Common Delay Causes

Severe Weather

Hurricanes, snowstorms, floods — natural events that halt logistics networks.

Peak Season Volume

Black Friday, Christmas, and other high-demand periods increase carrier loads significantly.

Customs Hold (International)

Packages may be held for inspection or documentation review by customs authorities.

Incorrect/Incomplete Address

Always double-check your shipping address to avoid rerouting delays.

Inventory Backorder

Rare cases where an item is unexpectedly out of stock after order placement.

Our promise: We will never knowingly delay your order without communicating. Transparency is at the core of our shipping practices.

SECTION 06

Lost Package Support

While rare, packages can occasionally go missing. Our dedicated support team ensures you're never left without resolution.

01

Report the Issue

Contact us within 14 days of the expected delivery date via email, live chat, or phone. Include your order number and tracking ID.

Email: support@goglobalmart.com
02

Investigation Begins

We open a formal inquiry with the carrier within 24 hours. You'll receive a case reference number and an estimated resolution timeline.

Typical investigation: 3-7 business days
03

Resolution Options

Once the carrier confirms the package is lost, you choose your preferred resolution:

  • Full reorder at no cost (expedited shipping free)
  • Full refund to original payment method
  • Full refund + 10% store credit (goodwill)
04

Resolution Delivered

Your chosen resolution is processed within 48 hours of confirmation. Refunds appear in 3-5 business days. Reorders ship within 24 hours.

100% satisfaction guaranteed on lost packages

Important: Reporting Window

Lost package claims must be filed within 14 days of the estimated delivery date for domestic orders, and 30 days for international orders. Claims filed after these windows may not be eligible for resolution. We strongly recommend enabling SMS/email alerts for the fastest notification of any delivery issues.

Quick answers to the most common shipping questions we receive.

Yes, we ship to P.O. boxes via USPS. However, express and overnight shipping options are not available for P.O. box addresses. For standard shipping, the carrier will typically leave the package at your door or a safe location. For express and overnight shipments requiring a signature, the carrier will attempt delivery up to 3 times over consecutive business days. After 3 failed attempts, the package will be held at the nearest carrier facility for 5 business days before being returned to us.
Customs fees vary by country and product category. While we can't predict exact amounts, most countries have a de minimis threshold (e.g., $800 for the US, €22 for the EU) below which no duties are charged. We recommend checking your country's customs website for specific thresholds. All international shipments include a customs declaration with accurate product descriptions and values.
Once an order has been shipped, it cannot be cancelled directly. However, you can request a return-to-sender intercept within the first 24 hours of shipment for a $5.00 intercept fee. Refunds for shipped orders are processed within 5-7 business days after we receive the returned items.
All domestic orders include basic carrier insurance (up to $100). Express and overnight shipments include coverage up to $500.
