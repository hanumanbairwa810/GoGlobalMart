How We Hire

Our Hiring Process

Transparent, respectful, and designed to find mutual fit. The entire process typically takes 2–3 weeks.

Step 01

Application Review

Our talent team reviews your application within 48 hours. We look at skills, experience, and cultural alignment — not just keywords.

⏱ 1–2 days
⏱ 3–5 days
Step 02

Screening Call

A 30-minute casual conversation with a recruiter. We discuss your background, motivations, and what you're looking for — no trick questions.

Step 03

Technical Assessment

A practical, take-home or live exercise relevant to your role. We focus on how you think and solve problems, not just the final answer.

⏱ 3–5 days
⏱ 2–3 days
Step 04

Team Interviews

Two 45-minute sessions with your future teammates and hiring manager. Conversations are collaborative — you're interviewing us too.

Step 05

Offer & Onboarding

If it's a match, you'll receive a competitive offer within 48 hours. Our onboarding is designed to set you up for success from week one.
