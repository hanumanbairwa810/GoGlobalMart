Your Privacy Matters

We are committed to protecting your data and being transparent about cookie usage.

Introduction

At GoGlobalMart Food, we believe in complete transparency about how we collect, use, and manage data through cookies and similar tracking technologies. This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, why we use them, and how you can control your cookie preferences.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device (computer, smartphone, or tablet) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, provide a better browsing experience, and supply information to the owners of the site. Cookies allow websites to recognize your device and remember information about your visit, such as your preferred language and other settings.

Why Do Websites Use Cookies?

Websites use cookies for a variety of reasons, including remembering your login details, keeping items in your shopping cart, analyzing how you interact with the site, and delivering personalized content. For a food delivery platform like GoGlobalMart Food, cookies are essential for ensuring a seamless ordering experience—from browsing restaurant menus to completing your checkout.

Importance for User Experience

Without cookies, you would need to re-enter your delivery address, payment preferences, and other settings every time you visit. Cookies help us remember your preferences, speed up the ordering process, and ensure that your food arrives exactly where and how you want it. They enable features like order tracking, saved favorites, and personalized restaurant recommendations.

How We Use Cookies

Keeping Users Logged In

Cookies remember your authentication state so you don't have to log in every time you visit. Your session stays active while you browse restaurants, add items to cart, and place orders.

Saving Cart Data

Your food selections, quantities, special instructions, and delivery preferences are stored via cookies so your cart remains intact even if you navigate away temporarily.

Improving Website Performance

Performance cookies help us identify bottlenecks, optimize page load times, and ensure that restaurant menus and images load quickly across all devices.

Personalized Experience

We use cookies to remember your cuisine preferences, dietary restrictions, favorite restaurants, and delivery address to tailor your experience every time you order.

Analytics & Traffic Tracking

We analyze aggregate traffic data to understand user behavior patterns, peak ordering times, popular cuisines, and geographic distribution—helping us improve our service.

Third-Party Cookies

In addition to our own cookies, GoGlobalMart Food may use cookies set by third-party services that appear on our pages. We do not control these cookies and recommend reviewing the privacy policies of these third-party providers.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to collect anonymized data about website usage. This helps us understand how visitors interact with our platform and identify areas for improvement. Data is aggregated and does not personally identify you.

Advertising Partners

Our advertising partners may use cookies to serve relevant ads based on your browsing behavior. These cookies help measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns and prevent repetitive ad displays.

Payment Gateway Cookies

When you make a payment, our payment processing partners (such as Stripe or PayPal) may set their own cookies to ensure secure transaction processing and fraud prevention.

Embedded Content & Services

Our pages may include embedded content from third-party services such as maps, social media widgets, or review platforms. These services may set their own cookies when you interact with embedded content.

Cookie Management

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. You can exercise your cookie preferences in several ways:

Cookie Consent Banner

When you first visit GoGlobalMart Food, a cookie consent banner appears allowing you to accept all, reject non-essential, or customize your cookie preferences.

Browser Settings

You can configure your browser to block or delete cookies through its settings. The process varies by browser—see the guides below.

Opt-Out Links

You can opt out of personalized advertising through the Digital Advertising Alliance (DAA) or the Network Advertising Initiative (NAI) websites.

Impact of Disabling Cookies

If you disable cookies, some features of GoGlobalMart Food may not function properly. You may experience issues such as being unable to log in, losing cart contents, not receiving personalized recommendations, and seeing irrelevant advertisements. Essential cookies cannot be disabled as they are necessary for the basic operation of our service.

Browser-Specific Cookie Settings

Google Chrome

Go to Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies and other site data. You can block, allow, or clear cookies for all sites or specific ones.

Mozilla Firefox

Go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data. Choose your preferred level of cookie blocking from Standard to Strict.

Apple Safari

Go to Preferences → Privacy. Use "Prevent cross-site tracking" and manage stored website data to control cookie behavior.

Microsoft Edge

Go to Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies. Block or allow cookies and set specific site permissions.

Data & Privacy Protection

GoGlobalMart Food is deeply committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that all cookie data is handled with the highest standards of security and compliance.

Privacy Commitment

We never sell your personal data to third parties. Cookie data is used solely to improve your experience and service quality.

Secure Data Handling

Browsing data collected through cookies is stored on secure servers with industry-standard protection measures and regular audits.

We comply with applicable data protection laws including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and other relevant privacy regulations. For more details, please review our complete Privacy Policy.
