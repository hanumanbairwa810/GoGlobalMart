1. Introduction

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by a website. Similar technologies, such as local storage, session storage and pixels, do comparable jobs. Websites use them to work properly, remember your choices, understand how the site is used, and, on shopping sites, to record referrals to partner stores.

GoGlobal Mart is a deal and price-comparison website that links to platforms such as Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho and Myntra. This policy explains what we use, or may use, and the choices available to you.

2. Types of cookies

Strictly necessary

Needed for security, basic site operation and features you ask for. These cannot be switched off in our settings and are not blocked when you reject optional cookies.

Preferences

Remember choices such as your cookie settings or saved items, so you do not have to repeat them.

Analytics

Measure traffic and performance. We will place analytics tools in this category only if they are installed, and only after consent where consent is required. [CONFIRM whether any analytics tool is installed.]

Advertising and affiliate technologies

Used by affiliate networks and retailers to attribute a purchase to a referral. Some of this happens on the partner's own website after you click a link, which we do not control.

Marketing

Used to build interest profiles or show tailored ads. [CONFIRM: none are currently used by GoGlobal Mart.] If this changes, they will be loaded only after your consent.

4. Third-party cookies

When third-party services are integrated or when you follow a link to a partner site, they may set their own cookies or use similar technologies. Examples of possible categories are analytics providers, payment services, affiliate networks, embedded content and advertising providers. Not every third party uses cookies, and we do not control those that do. They operate under their own policies, which may change. Outbound links on our site may pass through shortened or tracked links operated by our affiliate partners.

5. Cookie consent and preferences

Where optional cookies or similar technologies are used, you can accept all, reject non-essential, or choose by category. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time using the button below or the "Cookie Settings" link in the footer. Optional scripts controlled by this website are not loaded before you opt in, and a rejection never disables essential features.

Your choice is stored on your device and renewed after 12 months. [CONFIRM renewal period with legal counsel.] Your current choice: not set.

6. Managing cookies in your browser

Most browsers let you view, block or delete cookies in their privacy or settings menu. Blocking some cookies may stop parts of the site from working. Browser controls may not disable every tracking technology, and they will not remove your saved choice in localStorage unless you clear site data.
