Three experiences, three different models

The biggest difference is who completes the sale: an external seller, or GoGlobalMart.

Affiliate Shopping

You discover products on GoGlobalMart and may follow links to external seller websites to buy. The external seller generally handles payment, fulfillment, shipping and returns under its own policies. GoGlobalMart may earn a commission on qualifying purchases.

Mini Store

A direct-sale experience on GoGlobalMart. The ordering, payment, fulfillment and support steps are described below only once they are confirmed as implemented.

Food Restro

Food and restaurant-related services. Restaurant discovery, ordering, payment and delivery or pickup are described only once confirmed as available.

Important: an affiliate purchase is not a direct GoGlobalMart sale. Mini Store and Food Restro are direct-transaction sections, and aren't presented as operational unless they actually are.
