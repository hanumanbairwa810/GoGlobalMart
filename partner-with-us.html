Four ways to work with us

Each program has its own process and its own terms. They are not interchangeable, so choose the one that matches what you do.

Sell on GoGlobalMart Mini Store

Eligible third-party sellers can apply to list and sell their own products on the Mini Store marketplace.

Become a Seller
  • Seller application and product listing overview
  • Seller account review and verification, where implemented
  • Product moderation before products become publicly visible
  • Seller-managed packing and shipping, following the marketplace model
  • Seller agreement, marketplace commission and payout policies apply

Commission: the Mini Store model uses a 10% commission on successful orders. Eligibility begins when an order is marked delivered, and is subject to refunds, reversals and adjustments. The calculation base and some other financial details are not yet final, so the full terms will be shared in the seller agreement.

Brand and Affiliate Partnerships

Brands and merchants can explore having their products featured in the Affiliate Shopping section, subject to suitability and applicable affiliate program requirements.

Discuss a Brand Partnership
  • Review of your products and brand
  • Affiliate program and tracking compatibility, where applicable
  • Review of product information and promotional content
  • Clear disclosure of affiliate relationships where required (see our Affiliate Disclosure)

Placement, traffic, sales, commissions and conversions are not guaranteed. An affiliate partnership does not make a merchant a Mini Store seller.

Restaurant and Food Business Partnerships

Restaurants and food businesses can contact us to discuss possible collaboration with the Food Restro section. This is separate from the Mini Store seller program.

Partner With Food Restro
  • Restaurant or business details
  • Cuisine and menu information
  • Service location and operating hours
  • Food-business licensing or verification details, where legally applicable
  • Review of whether the business and the collaboration you want are supported

Tell us what you are looking for and we will confirm what is currently possible. Mini Store seller commission is not automatically applied to restaurants. Terms are discussed separately.

Business and Technology Collaborations

Propose a Collaboration

  • Logistics or delivery services, where relevant
  • Product and content collaborations
  • Strategic business proposals

Frequently asked questions

What partnership opportunities are available?

There are four tracks: Mini Store seller partnerships, brand and affiliate partnerships, restaurant and food business partnerships for Food Restro, and other business or technology collaborations. Each has its own process and terms.

How can I become a Mini Store seller?

Choose "Become a Seller" and send an inquiry. If you are approved to proceed, you will be guided through seller registration and shown the seller agreement before you start listing products.

Is every seller application automatically approved?

No. Seller accounts are reviewed, and products are moderated before they become publicly visible.

How are Mini Store seller commissions handled?

The Mini Store model uses a 10% commission on successful orders, with eligibility beginning when an order is marked delivered, subject to refunds, reversals and adjustments. The calculation base and some other financial details are not yet final. The final terms, including payout policy, will be set out in the seller agreement.

Can a brand discuss affiliate promotion?

Yes. Send a brand partnership inquiry. We consider suitability and applicable affiliate program requirements, and review product information and promotional content. We cannot promise placement, traffic, sales or commissions.

How can a restaurant explore Food Restro collaboration?

Send an inquiry with your business details, cuisine and menu, service location, operating hours and any licensing information. We will review whether the collaboration you want is supported. Food Restro is separate from the Mini Store seller program, and its terms are discussed separately.

Does submitting an inquiry guarantee a partnership?

No. An inquiry starts a review. Any partnership may require verification and a separate agreement.

What information should I include in my proposal?

Your business or brand name, website if you have one, location, what you sell or offer, what kind of partnership you want, and any relevant details such as product range, menu, licences or integration needs.

How will my submitted information be handled?

Your inquiry is handled as described in our Privacy Policy. Please share only business-related information.

How can I ask about an existing partnership application?

Use our Contact page and mention the business name and partnership type you applied under.
