6. Food Restro & Restaurant Discovery Services

Section C: Food Restro

The Food Restro section (`food-home.html`) connects customers with independent local dining partners and food vendors.

  • Intermediary Role: Unless verified as a proprietary culinary facility, GoGlobalMart acts as a digital intermediary connecting consumers with partner restaurants. We do not cook, prepare, or package food items.
  • Partner Responsibilities: Ingredient quality, hygiene standards, compliance with Food Safety and Standards (FSSAI) guidelines, and portion sizes are the sole responsibility of the preparing restaurant.
  • Delivery Estimates: Delivery windows (e.g., 20-30 mins) and travel distances are automated estimates subject to live traffic, weather, and kitchen preparation speed.

7. Pricing, Discounts, Deals & Real-Time Availability

Dynamic Pricing

Prices, discounts (such as "70% off"), promotional coupons, and inventory levels change dynamically across external marketplaces and food vendors.

Price Variance Notice
In the event of any difference between prices shown on GoGlobalMart and the final checkout cart on partner platforms (e.g., Amazon, Flipkart) or restaurant menus, the price displayed on the seller's final checkout screen takes precedence.

8. Food Allergies, Dietary Needs & Health Information

Critical Dietary Alert

When utilizing our Food Restro platform, please observe the following dietary guidelines:

  • Dietary Badges: Labels such as "Pure Veg", "Non-Veg", or "Healthy" reflect information declared by partner restaurants.
  • Allergens & Cross-Contamination: Partner kitchens prepare dishes in facilities handling nuts, gluten, dairy, soy, and eggs. Neither GoGlobalMart nor partner restaurants can guarantee an allergen-free environment unless confirmed by the restaurant.
  • Severe Allergies: If you have severe food allergies or medical dietary constraints, you must contact the restaurant directly before placing an order.
