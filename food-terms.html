Order & Payment Terms

Understanding the ordering and payment process is essential for a smooth experience. Below are the key terms governing orders and transactions on GoGlobalMart Food.

Order Confirmation

An order is confirmed only when you receive an official confirmation notification through the app or email. Pre-confirmation states do not guarantee order acceptance by the restaurant.

Payment Methods

We accept credit/debit cards, digital wallets, net banking, UPI, and cash on delivery (where available). All digital transactions are processed through secure, PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways.

Delivery Charges

Delivery fees vary based on distance, time of day, demand, and weather conditions. Peak hour surcharges may apply during high-demand periods. The exact fee is displayed before order placement.

Cancellation Policy

Orders may be cancelled within 2 minutes of placement at no charge. After the restaurant begins preparation, cancellation fees may apply. Orders cannot be cancelled once the delivery partner has picked up the food.

Refund Conditions

Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days for eligible cases including wrong orders, missing items, quality issues, or delivery failures. Evidence such as photos may be required to process refund claims.

Service Fee

A nominal platform service fee is applied to each order to cover technology, maintenance, and customer support costs. This fee is transparently displayed during checkout before you confirm your order.

GoGlobalMart Food facilitates food delivery through a network of independent delivery partners. The following terms govern the delivery process:

6.1 Estimated Delivery Times

Estimated delivery times provided on the Platform are approximations based on restaurant preparation time, distance, traffic conditions, and historical data. These estimates are not guaranteed and may vary due to factors beyond our control. We strive to provide the most accurate estimates possible.

6.2 Delivery Delays

Delivery delays may occur due to high order volumes, adverse weather conditions, traffic congestion, restaurant preparation delays, incorrect addresses, or other unforeseen circumstances. While we cannot guarantee delivery times, we continuously work to minimize delays. In cases of significant delays, customers may contact support for resolution options.

6.3 Failed Delivery Attempts

If a delivery partner is unable to complete delivery due to customer unavailability, incorrect address, refused acceptance, or inaccessible delivery location, the order will be marked as failed. In such cases, the customer will not be eligible for a refund, and a re-delivery fee may be charged for any subsequent attempt.

6.4 Delivery Zone Limitations

Delivery availability is subject to geographic limitations. Not all restaurants deliver to all areas, and delivery zones may be adjusted periodically based on operational feasibility. The Platform will clearly indicate whether a specific address falls within a restaurant's delivery zone before an order is placed.
