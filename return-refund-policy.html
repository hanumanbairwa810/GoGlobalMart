Introduction

This page explains how returns, cancellations and refunds work across GoGlobal Mart. We run three different kinds of experience, and they do not share one transaction system. Your options depend on whether you:

  • followed an affiliate link and paid an external seller,
  • ordered directly from the Mini Store, or
  • placed an order through Food Restro.

Read the section that matches your order. If you are not sure which applies, contact us and we will help you find the right place to go.

Affiliate Shopping: purchases on external sites

Seller or platform handles the order

Products listed under Affiliate Shopping link to third-party seller or marketplace websites. When you finish a purchase on one of those sites, the order, payment and delivery are handled by that seller, and its own return, refund, cancellation, warranty and delivery terms apply.

  • GoGlobal Mart does not process the payment for these purchases and generally cannot issue refunds for them.
  • For returns, cancellations or refunds, contact the seller or platform that accepted your order and payment. Use the order confirmation or your account on that site.
  • Not every seller accepts returns or offers refunds for every product. Check the seller's policy on the product page before you buy.
  • You can contact us if a product link is broken, or if the price or details shown on GoGlobal Mart look wrong or out of date.

For how we earn from these links, see our Affiliate Disclosure.

Nothing in this section limits any right you have under applicable law, including rights against a seller or platform.

Food Restro: food orders

Rules depend on order status

Food is different from ordinary products. It cannot usually be sent back, so remedies are decided case by case, based on what went wrong, the status of the order, applicable law, and the published rules of the restaurant or platform involved. Opened, prepared or delivered food is not automatically excluded from a remedy.

Wrong, missing, damaged or spilled items

Report the problem as soon as you can after delivery. Include your order number and, if you can, a photo of the items and packaging.

Quality concerns

If food was not as described or its quality was clearly unacceptable, tell us what happened and when you received it. We review each report on its facts.

Food safety and allergy concerns

If you believe food made you unwell or contained an allergen it should not have, seek medical help first if needed. Then report it to us promptly with your order number, the item, and what happened, and keep any remaining food and packaging if it is safe to do so. We cannot promise that any food is free of allergens, so check ingredient details with the restaurant when you order.

How refunds are processed

For Mini Store and Food Restro orders that GoGlobal Mart accepted, the process works like this:

  1. You send a request through the Contact Us page, with your order number.
  2. We match it to the correct order.
  3. We review eligibility and any evidence you provided.
  4. We tell you our decision or the current status.
  5. If a refund is approved, we start it through the payment process used for your order.
  6. Your bank or payment provider may need additional time before the money shows in your account.

We do not mark a refund as complete until the payment transaction confirms it. Refund timing is not guaranteed.
