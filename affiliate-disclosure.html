What affiliate links are

An affiliate link is a web address that carries a tracking code. When you click it and buy something from the seller's website, the seller can see that you arrived from GoGlobalMart. If the purchase qualifies under that seller's rules, they may pay us a commission.

Not every link on our site is an affiliate link. Whether a link earns us money depends on the seller and on the arrangement we have with them.

How GoGlobalMart may earn commissions

We may earn a commission when you click a product or deal link and complete a qualifying purchase on the seller's website. Qualifying rules, commission rates, and time limits are set by each seller or affiliate network, and they can change. A click does not guarantee that we earn anything.
