Verification Process

How We Verify Restaurants

Every restaurant goes through a rigorous 4-step verification process before joining our platform.

Step 1

Address Verification

Our team physically verifies the restaurant location, kitchen facilities, and ensures the address matches all business documentation.

Step 2

Phone Verification

Restaurant phone numbers are verified through OTP confirmation. We ensure a real person answers and can handle customer inquiries.

Step 3

Business Approval

Valid business licenses, food safety certifications, FSSAI/health department permits, and tax registration documents are reviewed.

Step 4

Quality Checks

Regular surprise inspections, food quality sampling, hygiene audits, and continuous monitoring of customer ratings and reviews.
