Our Story

Born from a Simple Idea

GoGlobalMart started in 2022 with a straightforward observation: millions of Indians deserve access to quality products at honest prices, but the existing platforms were either too expensive for sellers or too unreliable for buyers.

Founded by a small team of engineers and commerce enthusiasts in India, we set out to build a smart mini store marketplace — one that empowers local sellers with technology while giving buyers a seamless, trustworthy shopping experience.

Our mission is to make affordable shopping accessible to every household in India. We leverage AI for personalized recommendations, secure payment infrastructure for safe transactions, and a seller-first approach that keeps commissions low and support high.

Quick answers to the questions we hear most.

GoGlobalMart Mini Store is India's smart mini store marketplace that connects buyers and sellers globally. Register as a seller in minutes with your GST/PAN details. Create your mini store, list products with photos and descriptions, set your prices, and start selling. We offer low commission rates, a powerful analytics dashboard, and logistics support to help you scale.

Yes! GoGlobalMart delivers to 19,000+ pin codes across India, covering metro cities, Tier-2, and Tier-3 towns. We also ship to select international destinations, making it a true global marketplace for Indian products.
