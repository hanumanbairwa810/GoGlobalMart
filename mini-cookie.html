What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile phone) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, provide a better browsing experience, and supply information to the owners of the site.

At GoGlobalMart Mini Store, we use cookies to enhance your shopping experience, understand how our store is used, and deliver relevant content. Cookies allow us to recognize your device and remember information about your preferences or past actions.

Types of Cookies We Use

We categorize cookies based on their purpose and necessity. Below is a clear breakdown of each category used on GoGlobalMart Mini Store.

Strictly Necessary Cookies

Always On

These cookies are essential for the website to function properly. They enable core functionality such as page navigation, secure access to authenticated areas, and shopping cart management. The website cannot function properly without these cookies.

Cookie Duration Purpose
gogm_session Session Maintains your session while browsing
gogm_cart 30 days Stores items in your shopping cart
gogm_cookie_consent 1 year Records your cookie consent preferences
XSRF-TOKEN Session Prevents cross-site request forgery attacks

Functional Cookies

Optional

These cookies enable enhanced functionality and personalization, such as remembering your language preference, currency selection, and display settings. They may be set by us or by third-party providers whose services we have added to our pages.

Cookie Duration Purpose
gogm_locale 1 year Remembers your language and region
gogm_currency 1 year Stores your preferred currency

Analytics Cookies

Analytics cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting and reporting information anonymously. These insights allow us to improve the structure, content, and performance of GoGlobalMart Mini Store.

We use Google Analytics 4 as our primary analytics tool. Data is processed in anonymized form — we do not use analytics cookies to personally identify you.

Google Analytics 4

Optional

Measures site traffic, user journeys, and conversion events. All data is anonymized using Google's IP anonymization feature.

Cookie Duration Purpose
_ga 2 years Distinguishes unique visitors
_ga_* 2 years Maintains session state for GA4
_gid 24 hours Distinguishes unique visitors within a day
IP Anonymization: We have enabled IP anonymization in Google Analytics, meaning your IP address is truncated before being processed. This ensures compliance with GDPR's data minimization principle.

Advertising Cookies

Advertising cookies are used to deliver advertisements that are relevant to you and your interests. They also help limit the number of times you see an ad and measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns.

These cookies track your browsing activity across different websites and may be set by third-party ad networks with our permission. We work with the following advertising partners:

Meta (Facebook) Pixel

Optional

Measures the effectiveness of ads by tracking actions taken on our website after viewing a Facebook or Instagram ad.

Cookie Duration Purpose
_fbp 90 days Stores and tracks visits across websites
_fbc 2 years Tracks ad click attribution

Google Ads Remarketing

Optional

Enables remarketing to previous visitors of GoGlobalMart Mini Store across the Google Display Network.

Cookie Duration Purpose
__gads 13 months Enables ad personalization across Google
Opt-out links: You can opt out of personalized advertising directly through Google Ads Settings and Facebook Ad Preferences.

Your Control Options

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the ePrivacy Directive, you have the right to decide whether or not to accept cookies. Here are all the ways you can manage your preferences:

1

Cookie Consent Banner

When you first visit our site, a consent banner appears at the bottom of your screen. You can Accept All, Reject All, or click Customize to choose which categories to enable. Your choice is saved for 1 year.

2

Cookie Settings Button

At any time, click the "Cookie Settings" button in the navigation bar to reopen the preference center and modify your choices.

3

Browser Settings

Most browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can set your browser to refuse all cookies, accept only first-party cookies, or delete cookies when you close your browser. Note that disabling cookies may affect some website functionality.

4

Opt-Out Platforms

You can opt out of tracking across multiple websites using industry-standard tools:

NAI Opt-Out DAA Opt-Out EDMA Choices
5
