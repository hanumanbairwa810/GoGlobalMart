Introduction

Welcome to GoGlobalMart Food's Privacy Policy. This policy outlines how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use our food delivery platform.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal information is handled in a safe and responsible manner. This Privacy Policy applies to all users of our website, mobile application, and related services.

This policy describes our privacy practices with respect to information we collect through our platform, including when you place orders, create an account, or interact with our services in any way.

Information We Collect

To provide you with our food delivery services, we may collect the following types of information:

Personal Information

Your name, email address, and phone number to create and manage your account.

Delivery Information

Your delivery address to ensure accurate and timely delivery of your orders.

Order Details

Information about your food orders, including items selected, order history, and preferences.

Payment Information

Payment-related details necessary to process your transactions securely.

Device Information

Information about the device you use to access our services, including browser type and operating system.

Usage Data

How you interact with our platform, including pages visited, features used, and time spent.

How We Use Information

We use the information we collect for various purposes to provide and improve our services:

  • Processing Orders: To fulfill your food orders, process payments, and facilitate delivery to your specified location.
  • Improving Services: To analyze usage patterns and feedback to enhance our platform's functionality and user experience.
  • Customer Support: To respond to your inquiries, resolve issues, and provide assistance when needed.
  • Notifications & Updates: To send you order confirmations, delivery updates, and important information about your account or our services.
  • Fraud Prevention: To detect and prevent fraudulent activities, unauthorized access, and other security issues.
  • Analytics: To understand how users interact with our platform and make data-driven improvements.

Payment & Security

We prioritize the security of your financial information and implement robust measures to protect your transactions:

  • Secure Payment Gateways: We partner with trusted payment processors that adhere to industry security standards.
  • Encrypted Transactions: All payment information is encrypted using SSL technology during transmission.
  • Account Security: We implement measures to protect your account, including secure password requirements and optional two-factor authentication.
  • Fraud Protection: Our systems monitor for suspicious activity to prevent unauthorized transactions.

Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience on our platform:

  • Cookies Usage: We use cookies to store information about your preferences and past interactions with our platform.
  • User Preferences: Cookies help us remember your settings, such as language preferences and location settings.
  • Analytics Tracking: We use analytics tools to understand how users interact with our platform and improve our services.
  • Session Management: Cookies help maintain your session while you're logged in, ensuring a seamless experience.

Cookie Notice

By continuing to use our platform, you consent to the use of cookies in accordance with our Cookie Policy. You can manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings.

Data Sharing

We may share your information in the following circumstances:

  • Restaurant Partners: We share order details with restaurant partners to fulfill your food orders accurately.
  • Delivery Services: Delivery-related information is shared with delivery personnel to ensure successful delivery of your orders.
  • Third-Party Service Providers: We may share information with trusted third parties who assist us in operating our platform, processing payments, or analyzing data.
  • Legal Compliance: We may disclose information when required by law or to protect our rights, property, or safety, or that of our users or others.

User Rights

You have certain rights regarding your personal information:

Access Personal Data

You have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you.

Request Corrections

You can request corrections to any inaccurate or incomplete personal information we have.

Delete Account/Data

You have the right to request deletion of your account and associated personal data.

Manage Notifications

You can control the types of notifications you receive through your account settings.

Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16 years of age.

If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 16 without verification of parental consent, we will take steps to remove that information from our servers.

Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy:

  • Data Storage: Your information is stored on secure servers with appropriate security measures in place.
  • Order History: We retain your order history for a reasonable period to provide better service and resolve any potential issues.
  • Security Practices: We implement industry-standard security practices to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or destruction.

Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about how we are protecting your information. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.

Your continued use of our platform after any changes to this Privacy Policy constitutes your acceptance of such changes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my payment information secure? ▼

Yes, we take the security of your payment information very seriously. We use industry-standard encryption technology (SSL) to protect your payment data during transmission. We also partner with trusted payment processors that comply with PCI DSS security standards to ensure your transactions are secure.

Can I delete my account? ▼

Yes, you can request to delete your account by contacting our customer support team. Once your request is processed, your account and associated personal data will be permanently deleted from our systems, except where we are required to retain certain information for legal or operational reasons.

How is my address used? ▼

Your delivery address is used solely for the purpose of delivering your food orders. We share this information with our delivery partners only when necessary to complete your delivery. We do not use your address for marketing purposes without your explicit consent.

Do you share my data with third parties? ▼

We may share your data with third parties only as described in our Privacy Policy. This includes sharing order details with restaurant partners to fulfill your orders, with delivery personnel to complete deliveries, and with trusted service providers who help us operate our platform. We do not sell your personal information to third parties for their marketing purposes.
